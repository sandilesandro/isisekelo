Isisekelo Produce Farm (IPF) is a 100% Black-owned, Level-1 B-BBEE EME agricultural enterprise powered by visionary black female leadership headquartered in Mbombela, Mpumalanga. We bridge the gap between traditional farming wisdom and modern horticultural technology. Our mission is to stabilize the provincial food value chain by producing high-demand vegetables specifically cabbage, lettuce, and bell peppers using climate-resilient, sustainable practices.

02 / Operational Strategy

Precision Crop Cultivation

Isisekelo Produce Farm utilizes intensive horticultural practices to provide Mpumalanga with high-quality, nutrient-rich fresh produce under certified black female leadership.

🥬

Leafy Greens

Focusing on high-yield Drumhead Cabbage and Crisphead Lettuce. Our staggered planting ensures a reliable year-round supply for regional markets.

  • Drip irrigation for water efficiency
  • Organic-integrated pest management
  • Post-harvest cooling for freshness
🍅

Vine-Ripened Tomatoes

Cultivating Determinate and Indeterminate varieties optimized for brix content and shelf-life, targeting both retail and processing sectors.

  • Trellising for vertical yield max
  • Precision fertigation schedules
  • Strict blight & pest monitoring
🫑

Premium Peppers

Cultivating premium bell peppers in controlled environments to ensure consistent size, color, and flavor profiles for Nelspruit’s retail sector.

  • Net-house protection from hail
  • Soil nutrient monitoring
  • Direct-to-Retail supply chain

04 / Market & Social Impact
